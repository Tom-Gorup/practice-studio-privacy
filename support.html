Support

For help with the tuner, metronome, reference tones, or analysis, email support@cybrpulse.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of the issue. Thomas Gorup handles app support through this routed address. Please do not send an audio recording unless you choose to and it is needed to explain the issue.

Microphone access

The tuner and analysis need microphone access. Tap Start tuning or Start listening, then allow access when iOS asks. To change permission later, open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and enable Practice Studio.

No sound from tones or metronome

Check the iPhone volume and audio output, then stop and start the tone or metronome again. If the issue continues, tell us your device and iOS version.
