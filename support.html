Support

Need help with the tuner, metronome, reference tones, or analysis? A dedicated support address will be added before publication. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Microphone access

The tuner and analysis need microphone access. Tap Start tuning or start analysis, then allow access when iOS asks. To change permission later, open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and enable Practice Studio.

No sound from tones or metronome

Check the iPhone volume and audio output, then stop and start the tone or metronome again. If the issue continues, email us with your device and iOS version.
