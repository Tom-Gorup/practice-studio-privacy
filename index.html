Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Practice Studio is an iPhone app for tuning instruments, keeping time, playing reference tones, and viewing live sound analysis. Thomas Gorup develops and supports the app. Its current Apple App Store seller is CHAPPIE LLC; an individual seller transfer has not been completed. The CybrPulse domain hosts these information pages and support email addresses.

Microphone

The app requests microphone access when you start the tuner or live analysis. Microphone audio is processed on your iPhone to calculate pitch and show live analysis. The app does not record, save, or send microphone audio to the developer or a third party. You can stop listening in the app or change its permission in iPhone Settings.

App settings and storage

Practice Studio does not require an account and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking. Tuning and sound choices and metronome presets are stored locally on your iPhone. The app has no cloud sync or developer-operated data service. Saved choices remain until you change them or remove the app and its data. Offloading an app can retain its data, and iPhone backups may include local app data under your Apple settings.

This website and email

Opening a privacy or support link loads this separate website in your browser. The pages have no form, marketing cookies, or analytics script. The website host and DNS provider may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver the pages. If you email us, email and routing providers process the address and message you send so Thomas can reply. Please avoid sending recordings or other sensitive information unless it is needed to explain an issue.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, email privacy@cybrpulse.com. For product help, email support@cybrpulse.com. This policy describes Practice Studio version 1.0 and will be updated if its data practices change.
