# Practice Studio Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Practice Studio is an iPhone app for tuning instruments, keeping time, playing reference tones, and viewing live sound analysis.

## Microphone

The app requests microphone access when you start the tuner or live analysis. Microphone audio is processed on your device to calculate pitch and show the analysis displays. The app does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio. You can stop microphone use in the app or change its permission in iPhone Settings.

## Data and network use

The app does not require an account, display ads, or include analytics. It does not send personal information or audio off your device. Practice settings and metronome presets are saved locally on your device.

For privacy questions, email privacy@cybrpulse.com. For app support, email support@cybrpulse.com.

This policy describes version 1.0 of Practice Studio. Update it if the app's data practices change.
